Travel notes

✈️ Before you fly ›
  • By Wed 28 Oct: Check Splitwise and clear any pending balances
  • Check your travel insurance is active. Save the policy details on your phone
  • 72 hrs before landing: Fill the Vietnam Digital Arrival Card (free, official site). Address: The Summer Fortress Villa, Nhà Bè. Screenshot the QR code for immigration
  • Spirits allowance is 1.5 L per person, so plan accordingly
  • Pack closed shoes + clothes that can get dirty (Cu Chi tunnels), plus one smart-casual outfit (e.g. M Bar, Tue 3 Nov)
  • Download Google Translate with Vietnamese offline and Google Maps offline for the Ho Chi Minh City area. Handy when you're out of signal
🛬 When you land ›
  • Have your e-visa and arrival card QR ready for immigration
  • Get a local SIM at arrivals (Viettel counter), or set up an eSIM before you fly. Recommended if you're staying 3+ days
  • Install Grab. It's how we get around
  • Cash: use a VPBank ATM (no ATM fee, up to 10M VND per withdrawal). Always choose VND on screen, not your home currency
  • Quick VND → ₹ maths: drop 3 zeros, × 4, minus 10%
    • 10k ≈ ₹37 · 50k ≈ ₹180 · 100k ≈ ₹370
    • 500k ≈ ₹1,830 · 1M ≈ ₹3,670
  • Landing around the same time as others? Wait at the Viettel SIM counter and share a Grab to the villa
🏍️ During the trip ›
  • Tipping, not required but welcome for good service:
    • Tour guides: 100k–150k VND each (group tours)
    • Tour drivers: 50k–100k VND
    • Spa / massage: 50k–150k VND, hand to your therapist
    • Sit-down restaurants: 5–10%, only if no service charge
    • Grab & street food: not expected. Round up if you like
  • Every day: carry some cash and a photo of your passport
  • Your last night: check your "Leave by" time on the Crew tab and pack before going out
  • Throughout: log anything you pay for the group in Splitwise → Saigon 2026
🏠 After the trip ›
  • By Sun 15 Nov: Open Splitwise, tap Settle up, and pay whatever it shows

Practical tips
  • Most hospitals require upfront payment without insurance — carry your policy number
  • Pharmacies sell most medications over the counter — try one first for mild stomach issues
  • Call the hospital directly, not 115 — they speak English and send their own ambulance
  • Typical ER visit: $80–$120 (IV + blood tests). Hospitalization: $200–$500/day
